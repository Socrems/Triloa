# Community Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

We welcome participation in English or Chinese, regardless of background, identity, or experience. Help others participate by being respectful, patient, and constructive. Discuss ideas and technical decisions without personal attacks.

欢迎使用中文或英文参与，无论背景、身份或经验如何。请保持尊重、耐心和建设性，让他人能够安心参与；讨论观点与技术决定时，不作人身攻击。

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing another person's private information without permission are unacceptable. Respect boundaries and stop unwanted contact when asked.

不接受骚扰、威胁、歧视性辱骂、不受欢迎的性关注，或未经允许公开他人的私人信息。请尊重个人边界，在被要求停止联系时停止。

This applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project community spaces. Maintainers may remove or edit harmful content, close or lock discussions, or restrict participation in response to misconduct. Responses depend on the context and severity.

本准则适用于本仓库的议题、拉取请求、评审及其他项目社区空间。维护者可根据情况与严重程度，删除或编辑有害内容、关闭或锁定讨论，或限制参与。

For a non-sensitive concern, you may flag the relevant public thread for maintainers. A private project channel for conduct reports has not been established. For sensitive conduct concerns, use [GitHub's abuse reporting channel](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse); do not post private details publicly. That channel is operated by GitHub, and this project cannot promise its response or outcome. Suspected software vulnerabilities follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).

对于不涉及敏感信息的行为问题，可在相关公开讨论中提醒维护者。目前尚未建立项目专用的私密行为举报渠道。涉及敏感信息时，请使用 [GitHub 滥用举报渠道](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse)，不要公开私人细节。该渠道由 GitHub 运营，本项目无法承诺其回应或处理结果。软件漏洞请按单独的[安全政策](SECURITY.md)处理。
